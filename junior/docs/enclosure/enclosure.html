Busster Junior Enclosure

One aluminium sheet with a single bend, back and sloped control face, on four wooden wedge ribs, with a decorative wooden front rail and a plywood base. Drawn to scale; all sizes in centimetres, switch positions are hole centres. The wood under the sheet is shown dashed.

Window 11 x 6.7 cm for a 5" 800x480 IPS panel (Sunton ESP32-8048S050C), active area 10.8 x 6.5 cm, module about 12.1 x 7.6 cm (dashed, to confirm from the board drawing). The window's left edge is on switch 3's centre line, 17.1 cm from the left, 2 cm below the bend. Song switches are level with the window centre. The module clears the nearest LED hole by 2.1 cm and the nearest rib by 1.5 cm. Switches 1 and 8 are 5 cm from the panel ends, which leaves 0.3 cm between the switch body and the outer rib; that is why the panel is 52 wide rather than 48. The circuit board bolts to the inside of the back next to the jacks, so every electrical part is on the sheet and lifts out with it; wiring to the switches runs through holes in the inner ribs.
